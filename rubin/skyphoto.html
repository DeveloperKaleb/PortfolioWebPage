Stars in your photo

A test: take a photo of the night sky, outside after dark, and this page finds its stars and names them. Your photo stays on your phone; nothing is uploaded.

Point your phone at the night sky and find out which stars you're seeing.

How it works

Aim and shoot uses your camera, compass and location to work out where the phone is pointing, so even one or two stars can be enough. It takes about a second, so hold still, or lean the phone on something.

You can also choose a photo you've already taken. That needs more stars showing (six or more), because it goes by their pattern alone.

Everything happens here on your phone; nothing is uploaded. After dark the page turns red, which keeps your eyes used to the dark. The Night mode button switches it.

Or, for a sky with only a few stars showing: Uses your camera, compass and location, here on your phone only.
